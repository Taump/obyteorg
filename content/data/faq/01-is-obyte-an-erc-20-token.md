---
question: Is Obyte an ERC-20 token?
---

No, Obyte is a novel platform with its own DAG-based structure, consensus mechanism and eco system.

The native currency of Obyte platform is Bytes (traded as GBYTE on exchanges). Bridged versions of GBYTE are available as ERC-20 tokens on <a href="https://etherscan.io/token/0x31F69dE127C8A0fF10819C0955490a4Ae46fcc2a" target="_blank">Ethereum</a>, <a href="https://bscscan.com/token/0xeb34De0C4B2955CE0ff1526CDf735c9E6d249D09" target="_blank">BNB Smart Chain</a>, <a href="https://polygonscan.com/token/0xAB5F7a0e20b0d056Aed4Aa4528C78da45BE7308b" target="_blank">Polygon</a>, and <a href="https://kavascan.com/token/0x0b93109d05Ef330acD2c75148891cc61D20C3EF1" target="_blank">Kava chain</a>. They are bridged using the decentralized <a href="https://counterstake.org" target="_blank">Counterstake brridge</a>.
