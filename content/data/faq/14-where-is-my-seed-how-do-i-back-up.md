---
question: Where is my seed? How do I back up?
---

There are two backup options that you can find in the wallet's settings (under burger menu): backup of wallet seed and full backup. Backup of wallet seed allows you to recover only single-sig wallets while a full backup recovers everything including [Blackbytes](/platform/blackbytes), multi-sig wallets, [smart contracts](/platform/smart-contracts), [private identity profiles](/platform/identity), and [chat](/platform/chatbots) history and contacts.
