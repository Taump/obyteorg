    <Text style="block">
      <a href="https://www.worldcommunitygrid.org/" target="_blank">World Community Grid</a> is an IBM sponsored philanthropic initiative running since 2004. WCG allows one to use idle time of one's computer to help scientists solve the world’s biggest problems in health and sustainability.
    </Text>

    <Lead>
      One of the longest running distribution methods is by donating computing power to the World Community Grid.
    </Lead>
  </PageIntro>

  New users who join the Obyte WCG distribution are rewarded in Bytes for their previously accumulated points. Further contributions are rewarded with non-transferrable "WCG Points" tokens to recognize their contribution.

  <a href="https://www.worldcommunitygrid.org/team/viewTeamInfo.do?teamId=R1RD1XTFK92" target="_blank">Obyte team on WCG</a> is one of the top contributors by <a href="https://www.worldcommunitygrid.org/stat/viewStatsByTeamY.do?sort=results" target="_blank">results</a>, <a href="https://www.worldcommunitygrid.org/stat/viewStatsByTeamY.do?sort=points" target="_blank">points</a>, and <a href="https://www.worldcommunitygrid.org/stat/viewStatsByTeamY.do?sort=cpu" target="_blank">total run time</a>, and has contributed over 29,000 years of run time since the program started. We have participated in multiple challenges and won some of them, e.g. <a href="https://www.worldcommunitygrid.org/team/challenge/viewTeamChallenge.do?challengeId=9589" target="_blank">THOR Challenge 2019</a>.

  Learn more about the <a href="https://blog.obyte.org/computing-for-good-again-3795336bdaed" target="_blank">distribution through WCG</a>.
